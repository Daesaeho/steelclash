# Two-handed mob grip — 2026-10-14

## Reproduction and correction

The player reach checkpoint did not integrate the returned support rig into `MobCombatPoses`: turnable mobs used
the solved weapon arm, then recomputed their support arm with a fixed shoulder through `pose.gripArm`. Native husk
evidence reproduced handle contact in only 22/40 full-weight poses. Accounting for the husk renderer's 1.0625 scale,
the support palm missed its intended target by up to four model pixels.

Turnable two-handed mobs now use `WeaponRig.offArm` and the already mirrored `offShoulder`, weighted with the
combat pose. Unknown item renderers retain the existing pitched-arm fallback; this does not assert grip support for
their custom attachments. One-handed poses and occupied offhands retain their existing path.

The first live morph revealed a second defect absent from frozen shots. Vanilla `HumanoidModel.setupAttackAnimation`
moves the arm pivots with `attackTime`, whereas the solver uses canonical shoulder pivots. A real slash-to-stab morph
therefore still missed the support target by up to 1.995665 model pixels during release. Two-handed turnable mobs now
blend both shoulder X/Z pivots into the solver's frame before adding the support translation. Their common vanilla
Y height is retained for crouching/riding. A NeoForge `RenderLivingEvent.Post` handler restores both original arm
positions after the draw, so shared renderer models do not retain those translations for other entities.

Attack timing, reach, hit tracing, packets and vanilla damage dispatch are unchanged. The native sword authoring
candidate remains an external QA override; it has not replaced the shipped animation resources.
